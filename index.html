Who We Are

Built for Champions.
Powered by Community.

LEVELYN Esports is a visionary organization dedicated to shaping the future of competitive gaming. Founded on December 23, 2023, we have rapidly grown into one of Bangladesh's most trusted esports communities.

We prioritize professionalism, innovation, and inclusivity — ensuring every member has the tools to grow, compete, and thrive. Whether you're a seasoned pro or a passionate newcomer, LEVELYN is where you level up.
