Who We Are

Built for Champions.
Powered by Community.

LEVELYN Esports is a visionary esports organization dedicated to shaping the future of competitive gaming. Founded on December 23, 2023, we have rapidly grown into one of Bangladesh's most exciting esports communities.

With a commitment to fostering talent, creating opportunities, and building a global gaming community, LEVELYN provides players with the platform they need to succeed — whether you're a seasoned pro or a passionate newcomer.
